              A few quick answers

              What should I report?

              Broken links, incorrect details, missing clubs, confusing matches, or anything that stops you using the site. Specific steps make an issue easier to reproduce.

              Why do related activities appear?

              Direct matches come first. Related activities share a topic or discovery tag and are labeled separately, so you can explore beyond the exact words you searched.

              Where are my bookmarks stored?

              In this browser on this device. Clearing site data removes them, and they do not sync between devices. If storage is unavailable, a notice explains that new saves last only for your current visit.

              Are hours and openings live?

              No. Listings are a reviewed collection with official links. Check those sources for current access, hours, availability, and application details.

              Who reads my report?

              The student maintaining this independent project. FormSubmit processes the report for email delivery. Reports are not added to the public catalog.
