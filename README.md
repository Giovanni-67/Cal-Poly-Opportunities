# Cal Poly Opportunity Matcher

Find your next way to get involved at Cal Poly.

Cal Poly Opportunity Matcher brings clubs, research programs, competitions, hands-on projects, and career resources into one searchable campus guide. It helps students turn an interest or major into a starting point for building experience.

**[Explore the app](https://giovanni-67.github.io/Cal-Poly-Opportunities/)**

## What you can do

- **Discover opportunities.** Search by major or interest, combine interests, and filter by opportunity type. Each result includes a match explanation and an official source link.
- **Explore related activities.** Look beyond exact matches with a separate set of related suggestions. A search for swimming, for example, can lead to other water activities.
- **Find study spots.** Browse campus locations and narrow them by features, with location and access notes.
- **Navigate campus resources.** Find academic support, career services, research resources, and places to make or build things.
- **See upcoming career events.** Browse a dated agenda of curated fairs and company events, with official details and routes to employer lists.
- **Save a shortlist.** Bookmark opportunities, study spots, and resources, then search your saved collection.

## How it works

Start with a major or something you enjoy, choose **Find Opportunities**, and open a result to learn more. Use **Browse all** whenever you want to explore the full collection. Majors and interests guide discovery; they do not limit what you can browse.

The interface adapts to desktop and mobile, with keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and support for reduced motion.

## About the collection

The catalog contains **429 curated entries**, including **406 San Luis Obispo student organizations** from the public directory snapshot reviewed in September 2026. Additional programs, study spots, resources, and career events have their own source notes and review dates.

Listings are a curated collection rather than a live availability feed. Confirm current details, deadlines, participation requirements, and event locations through the official links. Study spot information does not represent live seating or opening hours.

Bookmarks are saved in the current browser, without an account or cross-device sync. Some official services linked from the app require a Cal Poly sign-in.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with local search and browser-based bookmark storage. The app is published through GitHub Pages and checked with automated matching, storage, and browser regression tests.
