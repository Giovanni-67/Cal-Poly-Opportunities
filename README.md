# Cal Poly Opportunity Matcher

A campus discovery app that helps Cal Poly students find clubs, research, competitions, hands-on projects, and resources connected to their interests.

**[Explore the app](https://giovanni-67.github.io/Cal-Poly-Opportunities/)**

## Highlights

- **Search by major or interest.** Combine interests and filter by opportunity type, with explanations of why each result matches.
- **Explore beyond exact matches.** Discover related activities or browse the full collection without a major restriction.
- **Find places and support.** Browse study spots, academic services, career resources, and spaces for making and building.
- **Browse career events.** View a dated, curated agenda with official event details and links to employer information.
- **Keep a shortlist.** Bookmark opportunities, study spots, and resources in one searchable collection.

## The experience

Search, open a result, and follow its official link to learn more. Matching helps students find a starting point; it does not determine eligibility or restrict what they can explore.

The layout adapts to desktop and mobile, with keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.

## Sources and saved items

The opportunity catalog includes 429 curated entries, including 406 student organizations from a September 2026 public directory snapshot. Listings carry official source links and review notes.

This is a curated guide, not a live availability feed. Check official sources for current deadlines, participation requirements, event details, and opening hours. Study spots do not show live seating availability.

Browsing requires no account. Bookmarks stay in the current browser and do not sync across devices. Some linked campus services require a Cal Poly sign-in.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with local search, browser bookmark storage, and GitHub Pages hosting.

An independent project, unaffiliated with Cal Poly. Use **Report bugs** in the app's footer to report a problem.
